<objective>
Add per-segment asymmetric triple barrier configuration, with ru_energy getting wider downside barriers for commodity-linked volatility.

Purpose: Energy stocks experience asymmetric volatility — commodity-linked downside moves are sharper and wider than upside. Symmetric barriers cause premature lower barrier hits that generate noisy SELL labels. Wider downside barriers give energy positions more room during commodity selloffs.
Output: _SEGMENT_BARRIER_CONFIG dict + _get_barrier_params helper in both scripts, with tests.
</objective>
