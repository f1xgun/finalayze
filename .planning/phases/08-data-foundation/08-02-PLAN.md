<objective>
Exclude the Feb 21 - Apr 1 2022 MOEX structural break from vol/ATR calculations in the backtest engine so that volatility estimates are not distorted 3-5x by the MOEX closure and circuit breaker period.

Purpose: The MOEX was closed Feb 28 - Mar 24 2022, with extreme dislocation before/after. This distorts vol estimates 3-5x and teaches false mean-reversion patterns. Prices during this period must remain in OHLCV data for position tracking, but vol/ATR windows must skip these bars.

Output: BacktestConfig with exclude_periods field; stop_loss.py and engine.py filter candles for ATR/vol computation; tests proving exclusion works.
</objective>
